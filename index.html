A word from the owner

I started Gajanand Gifts one year ago with a simple idea: a gift feels special when it carries your own memory.

The shop is named after my family's faith and blessings, and my relatives were the first people to place orders. A birthday mug for a cousin, a T-shirt for a family trip. Their happy faces told me this was the right work to do.

Today people from Dawarpar and nearby areas come to us for mugs, magic mugs, T-shirts and photo gifts. Bring your photo, tell me the occasion, and I will make sure it is printed neatly and ready on time.
